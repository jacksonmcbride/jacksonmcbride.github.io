Privacy Policy — Feed Cleaner (Hide Ads & Suggested Posts)

This extension runs entirely inside your browser on the site it's installed for.

  • It does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any data.
  • It makes no network requests of its own.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, or third-party services.
  • All processing happens locally in the page and is discarded when you navigate away or close the tab.
